About the Book

What Creative Acquisitions Covers

A complete playbook for acquiring businesses using strategies that don't require millions in upfront capital.

The Problem

Most aspiring business owners believe they need massive capital, pristine credit, and years of industry experience to acquire a business. That belief keeps thousands of capable entrepreneurs on the sidelines while profitable businesses change hands every day through creative deal structures that bypass traditional financing entirely.

The Solution

Creative Acquisitions is the field manual for entrepreneurs who want to buy businesses using unconventional strategies. From seller financing and earn-outs to equity rollovers and assumption deals, this book covers every creative structure available to buyers who think differently about deal-making.

Who This Book Is For

First-Time Buyers

Entrepreneurs looking to acquire their first business but lacking the traditional capital or banking relationships to do it the conventional way.

Serial Acquirers

Experienced buyers looking to add creative deal structures to their toolkit and close deals that others walk away from.

Business Brokers

Professionals who want to understand how creative buyers structure deals so they can better serve both buyers and sellers.

Key Takeaways

Master Seller Financing

Learn how to negotiate seller-financed deals that work for both parties. Understand the psychology of sellers and how to present terms that get accepted.

Structure Earn-Outs That Align Incentives

Design earn-out agreements that protect you as the buyer while giving the seller confidence in the deal. Avoid the common pitfalls that blow up earn-out deals.

Build a Due Diligence System

A repeatable framework for evaluating businesses that uncovers both hidden risks and hidden value before you commit capital.

Negotiate Like a Professional Acquirer

Frameworks and scripts for negotiating business acquisitions. Handle objections, create urgency, and close deals on your terms.

Close Deals With Minimal Capital

Combine multiple creative strategies into financing stacks that allow you to acquire businesses with little to no money out of pocket.
