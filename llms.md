## Book Summary

Creative Acquisitions is a comprehensive guide to buying businesses using unconventional strategies. The book covers creative deal structures, seller financing, earn-outs, equity rollovers, assumption deals, and financing stacks that allow entrepreneurs to acquire profitable businesses without massive upfront capital. Every strategy is supported by real-world case studies showing how actual deals were structured, negotiated, and closed.

## Key Strategies Covered

- **Seller Financing:** Negotiating deals where the seller acts as the bank, providing favorable terms that bypass traditional lenders.
- **Earn-Out Structures:** Performance-based payment structures that reduce risk and align buyer-seller incentives.
- **Equity Rollovers:** Allowing sellers to retain a minority stake, reducing capital requirements and keeping them invested.
- **SBA 7(a) Loans:** Using government-backed loans for acquisitions with as little as 10% down.
- **No Money Down Methods:** Strategies for acquiring businesses with zero out-of-pocket capital using creative financing stacks.
- **Search Fund Model:** Raising capital from investors to search for and acquire a single business.
- **Roll-Up Strategy:** Acquiring multiple businesses in the same industry to create economies of scale and higher valuations.
- **Holdco Model:** Building a holding company structure for acquiring and operating multiple businesses.
- **Off-Market Deals:** Finding and acquiring businesses that are not publicly listed for sale.
- **Working Capital Pegs:** Structuring working capital agreements that protect both parties at closing.
