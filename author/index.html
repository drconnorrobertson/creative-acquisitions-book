About Dr. Robertson

Dr. Connor Robertson is an entrepreneur, investor, and author whose work sits at the intersection of business acquisition strategy and creative finance. With a background that spans academic research and hands-on deal-making, Dr. Robertson brings a unique perspective to the world of business acquisitions.

His mission is to democratize business ownership by teaching entrepreneurs how to acquire profitable businesses using creative deal structures that don't require massive upfront capital. Through his writing, speaking, and advisory work, he has helped countless entrepreneurs transition from aspiring business owners to active acquirers.
