12 Chapters

Chapter Overview

From finding your first deal to scaling post-acquisition, every chapter delivers actionable strategies.

01

The Acquisition Mindset

Why buying an existing business is one of the most powerful wealth-building strategies available, and how to shift your thinking from startup founder to strategic acquirer.

02

Finding Acquisition Targets

Where to find businesses for sale, how to source off-market deals, and the criteria that separate great acquisition targets from money pits.

03

Creative Deal Structures

An overview of every creative acquisition structure available: asset purchases, stock purchases, mergers, and hybrid models. When to use each and why.

04

Seller Financing Deep Dive

The mechanics of seller financing, how to present it to sellers, structuring terms that protect both parties, and the legal frameworks you need to know.

05

Earn-Out Agreements

How to design earn-out structures that align incentives between buyer and seller. Includes formulas, benchmarks, dispute resolution mechanisms, and real examples.

06

No Money Down Acquisitions

Strategies for acquiring businesses with zero cash out of pocket. SBA loans, seller financing stacks, equity rollovers, and assumption deals explained step by step.

07

Due Diligence Mastery

A systematic approach to evaluating acquisition targets. Financial, operational, legal, and market due diligence frameworks with checklists and red flags.

08

Valuation and Pricing

How to value small and mid-size businesses. Multiple methods, industry benchmarks, and how to use valuation as a negotiation tool rather than a static number.

09

Negotiation Tactics

Proven negotiation frameworks for business acquisitions. How to anchor, counter, handle objections, create urgency, and close deals on terms that work for you.

10

Legal and Tax Considerations

The legal and tax structures every acquirer must understand. Entity selection, asset vs. stock purchases, tax implications, and working with attorneys and CPAs.

11

Post-Acquisition Playbook

The first 90 days after closing. Employee retention, customer communication, operational improvements, and the systems that turn a good acquisition into a great one.

12

Building an Acquisition Portfolio

How to go from one acquisition to many. Roll-up strategies, platform acquisitions, bolt-on deals, and building a portfolio of cash-flowing businesses.
